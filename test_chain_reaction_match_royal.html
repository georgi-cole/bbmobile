Chain Reaction - Match Royal Style

Gameplay Rules:

  • Colored Tiles: Grid filled with colored tiles (no numeric counters)
  • Click Groups: Click contiguous same-color groups (4-way connected)
  • Group Size ≥2: Groups with 2+ tiles can be popped
  • Singles Blocked: When groups ≥2 exist, singles cannot be clicked
  • Singles Allowed: When only singles remain, they become clickable to finish
  • Gravity: Tiles fall down after pops; no automatic refill
  • 2 Rounds: Clear the board, get more tiles, clear again to win
  • Cell Size: Default 36px squares (smaller than previous version)
