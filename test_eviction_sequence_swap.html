Eviction Sequence Swap Test

Verifies that the faux TV animation plays BEFORE the red X appears on the roster

Test Overview

Expected Behavior

  1. Eviction announcement card appears (unchanged)
  2. Faux TV animation plays in the TV area (zoom-in → B&W → fade)
  3. During animation: Top roster shows NO red X on evicted player
  4. After animation completes: Red X appears on evicted player in roster

⚠️ Watch the roster carefully: The red X should NOT appear until AFTER the TV animation finishes!
