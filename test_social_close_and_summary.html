🎯 Test Objectives

This test validates:

  1. Close button (X) functionality when user has remaining energy
  2. Continue prompt appears when closing with energy > 0
  3. Summary layout is centered inside faux-TV container
  4. Text overflow is handled properly in summary

📋 Test Procedure

Test 1: Close with Remaining Energy
  1. Click "Setup Game State"
  2. Click "Open Social Modal"
  3. Click "Set Energy to 3"
  4. Click "Click Close (X) Button"
  5. Expected: Continue prompt appears with Yes/No options
  6. Click "No" - summary should appear centered in faux-TV
Test 2: Close with No Energy
  1. Click "Setup Game State"
  2. Click "Open Social Modal"
  3. Click "Set Energy to 0"
  4. Click "Click Close (X) Button"
  5. Expected: Modal closes immediately, NO prompt, summary appears
Test 3: Summary Layout
  1. Click "Show Summary (Manual)" with any energy level
  2. Expected: Summary card appears centered inside the blue faux-TV container below
  3. Check: Text doesn't overflow, card is compact, centered
