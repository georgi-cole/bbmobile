📋 Improvements Summary

  • Text Color: Changed from var(--card) to #f0f8ff (Alice Blue - white-blueish like TV overlays)
  • Text Shadow: Enhanced with 5-layer shadow for maximum glow and readability
  • Background: Increased opacity from rgba(60, 80, 100, 0.3) to rgba(40, 60, 80, 0.85)
  • Box Shadow: Slightly increased from 0.2 to 0.3 alpha for better depth
  • Separator: Increased opacity from 0.3 to 0.5 with additional shadow
  • Result: Text and timer are now highly readable at ALL progress levels, including 0% (depleted)
