# Social Maneuvers (Quick Guide)

## What is Social Maneuvers?

Social Maneuvers lets you spend **Energy** to interact with houseguests—chat, strategize, and shape relationships. Stronger relationships unlock more options and improve your chances of success in the house.

This phase appears periodically during the game, giving you opportunities to build alliances, gather information, and influence other players outside of competitions.

## When Does It Appear?

The Social Maneuvers phase occurs at specific points in each week's cycle, typically between major events like HOH competitions, nominations, veto, and eviction ceremonies. When active, you'll see a dedicated interface to interact with other houseguests.

## The Three Resources

Social Maneuvers uses three key resources that track your social standing and strategic knowledge:

### Energy
Your social stamina for the phase. Energy is used when you perform social actions. You accumulate Energy through weekly events and bonuses—success in competitions, avoiding nomination, and maintaining strong relationships all contribute. Energy carries over between weeks, so strategic play compounds over time.

### Influence
Your standing with specific houseguests. Influence improves as you build stronger relationships and successfully complete social interactions. Some advanced actions require higher Influence with certain players to unlock. Influence helps you persuade others and opens up strategic options.

### Information
Strategic knowledge you've gathered about the house dynamics. Information is earned through observation actions and successful strategic conversations. Some high-impact actions require Information to execute, representing that you need to know the right intel before making your move.

## How to Use Social Maneuvers

### Basic Flow

1. **Select Players**: Choose which houseguest(s) you want to interact with
   - **Mobile**: Tap a player card to select/deselect them
   - **Mobile Group Mode**: Long-press any card to toggle Group selection mode, then tap multiple cards
   - **Desktop**: Click to select one player, or Ctrl/Cmd-click to multi-select

2. **Pick an Action**: Browse available actions in the menu
   - The preview shows what it costs (Energy, Influence, Information)
   - It also indicates if the action is available or locked
   - **Active actions appear first** at the top of the list
   - Inactive actions appear below, showing why they're unavailable

3. **Execute**: Confirm your choice to see the outcome
   - Outcomes can improve relationships (increase Influence)
   - Some actions grant Information
   - Results vary based on your existing relationship with that player

### Action Requirements

Some actions need **enough rapport first**. For example:
- Basic conversations are always available
- Strategic discussions may require moderate Influence
- Advanced alliance-building actions need strong relationships

If an action is inactive, look at the requirements hint. You may need to:
- Build the relationship gradually with simpler actions first
- Accumulate more Influence through successful interactions
- Gather Information via observation

### Group Selection

You can select **multiple players** for group actions:
- Some actions work with groups (e.g., "Chat with everyone")
- Others are one-on-one only
- The interface shows which type each action supports

### Eviction Note

**Evicted players cannot use the Social Maneuvers module.** Once you're evicted from the house, you no longer participate in social phases. However, if you become a juror, you'll still influence the game by voting for the winner in the finale.

## Tips for Success

- **Build relationships gradually**: Start with simple conversations before attempting high-stakes strategic moves
- **Observe to gather info**: Use observation actions to collect Information when you need it
- **Use strategy actions when unlocked**: As your Influence grows, more powerful actions become available
- **Manage Energy wisely**: Energy accumulates over time, but each action costs some—prioritize the interactions that matter most
- **Strong relationships unlock options**: The better your rapport with someone, the more you can do with them

## Accessibility

- All player cards are buttons with clear labels
- Keyboard navigation is supported
- Hints explain controls (e.g., long-press for group mode on mobile)
- Visual indicators show selected players and available actions
