🎬 TV Overlay Blur Removal Test

Verifying that veto ceremony cards inside the TV have no blur/dim backdrop

✓ Changes Applied

Problem: Veto ceremony popups inside the faux TV had unwanted blur and dim effects that created a "blurred film" over the TV screen.

Solution: Neutralized blur/dim specifically for elements inside #tvOverlay while keeping fullscreen overlays intact.

  • Old: .tvDim had backdrop-filter: blur(4px) and gradient background
  • New: #tvOverlay .tvDim now has transparent background and no backdrop-filter
  • Old: .revealCard.diaryRoomCard had backdrop-filter: blur(12px) and box-shadow
  • New: #tvOverlay .revealCard.diaryRoomCard now has no backdrop-filter or box-shadow
