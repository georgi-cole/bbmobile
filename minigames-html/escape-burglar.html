Escape the Burglar — Grid Puzzle

You (🔵) must reach the exit (🚪) before the burglar(s) (🔴) catch you. Each move you make, burglars move one step closer using greedy pathfinding. Avoid walls (⬛). Complete more levels to face tougher challenges with larger grids and more burglars!

Mobile-first: swipe on the grid or tap arrow buttons. No external assets.
