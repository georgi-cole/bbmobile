🏆 Winner Announcement UI Test

Test the enhanced winner announcement with animated rainbow border and timed runner-up display.

Quick Test Actions

Expected Timing Sequence

t = 0.0s Winner appears with animated rainbow border
t = 2.0s Runner-up miniature appears (bottom-right)
t = 4.5s Runner-up fades out
t = 7.0s Winner screen closes

QA Checklist

  • Cyan border is pulled inward from viewport edges (not glued to sides)
  • Rainbow border animates smoothly with rotating gradient
  • Inner cyan glow is visible around winner card
  • Runner-up miniature is fully visible (not clipped)
  • Runner-up respects safe-area insets on iOS devices
  • Timing matches: 0s → 2s → 4.5s → 7s
  • Animation respects prefers-reduced-motion
  • Responsive design works on mobile and desktop viewports
