🔋 Zero Energy Modal Test

Test Scenario

This test demonstrates the new 0 social energy modal that appears when a player has no social energy at the start of the social phase.

Features:
  • 🔋 Empty battery icon with blinking animation
  • ⏭️ SKIP button - advances to next phase
  • 🎬 Recharge button - grants +5 energy, +5 influence, +5 information
