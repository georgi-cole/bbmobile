🎭 Eviction Modal Test Harness

This page tests the new eviction result modal system. The modal should appear centered and fully visible above all content, even if triggered from within the TV container.

Expected Behavior

  • Modal appears centered in viewport (not clipped by TV container)
  • High z-index (9000) ensures visibility above all content
  • Backdrop blur and semi-transparent overlay
  • Click backdrop or press ESC to close
  • Focus trapped within modal for accessibility
  • Auto-dismiss after specified duration

This simulates the TV container where evictions are shown.

The modal should appear ABOVE this container, fully visible.
