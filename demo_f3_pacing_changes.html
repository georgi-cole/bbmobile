🗣️ New Feature: Plea Panel

After the Final HOH is crowned, a new interactive phase allows nominees to make their final case:

Phase: final3_plea
Living-room ceremony theme with 🗣️ icon
↓
Human Nominee Scenario:
• Interactive FinalPlea modal opens
• Choose from 5 strategic plea options:
  1. "I'm a weaker competitor—take me and you'll win"
  2. "We've been allies all season—honor our bond"
  3. "The other player has a stronger jury case"
  4. "I'll vote for you in the finale if you save me"
  5. Custom message (up to 200 characters)
• Submit or skip plea
↓
AI Consideration:
• Plea influence stored in g.__f3PleaInfluence
• Calculated based on affinity, HOH personality, and argument strength
• Up to 20% swing in AI eviction decision
↓

⚙️ Settings Toggle

Users can toggle the optimization on/off for testing or preference:

Location: Settings → Timing → Final Week pacing
Setting Key: skipIdleTimersF3
Default: true (optimized pacing enabled)
Label: "Skip idle timers in Final Week (optimized UX)"
↓
When ENABLED (default):
✓ Short 1.4s instruction cards
✓ Full scoreboards in results modals
✓ No idle waits between phases
✓ Plea panel before final decision
↓
When DISABLED (legacy):
• Verbose 4.5s instruction cards
• No scoreboard modals
• Original idle waits preserved
• Direct to decision (no plea panel)
