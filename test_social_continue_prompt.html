Social Continue Prompt Test

This test validates the "continue socializing?" prompt after partial energy usage.

Test Steps:

  1. Click "Setup Game State" to initialize the game
  2. Click "Open Social Modal" to open the social interface
  3. Click "Set Energy to 3" to give yourself some energy
  4. Click "Close Modal" - you should see a prompt asking if you want to continue
  5. Test both "Yes" (should keep modal open) and "No" (should show summary) options
  6. Also test with "Set Energy to 0" - closing should NOT show the prompt
